<p>

<ul>

<li> <a href="https://abstractatlas.brainkb.org/ohbm2026/abstract/0379/">Poster 0379</a>: <a href="/assets/presentations/tedana_poster_OHBM2026_sm.pdf">Growing Tedana: a grassroots-leveraged platform for processing multi-echo fMRI data</a> - <a href="/members/handwerker">Dan Handwerker</a></li>
<li> <a href="https://abstractatlas.brainkb.org/ohbm2026/abstract/0746/">Poster 0746</a>: <a href="/assets/presentations/Marlene_OHBM_poster.pdf">What is the Optimal Number of ICA Components for fMRI Denoising?</a> -  <a href="/members/smith2">Marlene Smith</a></li>

<h2>Talks</h2>
In addition to the poster sessions, SFIM also gave talks! <br>

<ul>
<li>Sunday, June 14 (9:00AM-5:30PM): OHBM Education Course on Practical Multi-echo fMRI: Underlying principles, challenges, potential, and applications - <a href="/assets/presentations/Handwerker_Intro_and_course_overview_OHBM2026_sm.pdf">"An introduction to multi-echo fMRI and tedana"</a> -- <a href="/members/handwerker">Dan Handwerker</a> and <a href="/assets/presentations/">"Javier's talk title"</a> -- <a href="/members/gonzalez-castillo">Javier Gonzalez-Castillo</a></li>
<li>Monday, June 15 (10:45AM-12:00PM): <a href="/assets/presentations/OHBM26SpatialSimPrez.pdf">"Spatial maps of similarity across the cortex"</a>  - <a href="/members/faskowitz">Josh Faskowitz</a></li>
<li> Monday, June 15 (3:00PM): <a href="/assets/presentations/OHBM_2026_RRs_roundtable.pdf">"A Senior Editor bringing a constructive "devil’s advocate" perspective" - part of the "Registered Reports (RRs) in Neuroscience: Trainee-to-Editor Perspectives and Paths to Adoption" Roundtable</a> - <a href="/members/bandettini">Peter Bandettini</a></li>
<li>Tuesday, June 16 (5:30PM - 06:30PM): "From Data to Publication: Aperture Neuro’s Guidelines for Reproducible Research" - <a href="/members/bandettini">Peter Bandettini</a></li>
<li> Thursday, June 18 (6:30PM - 07:30PM): <a href="/assets/presentations/Neurosalience Live Audience Podcast 2026_June9.pdf">"OHBM Neurosalience Podcast: Bringing it all together - The need to unify scales, modalities, and tools through scientific scaffolding and overreaching theories.”</a> - <a href="/members/bandettini">Peter Bandettini</a></li>
